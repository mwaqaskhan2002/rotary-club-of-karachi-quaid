Profile Details

CURRENT PRESIDENT

Mr. Adnan Saeed is the Chief Operating Officer at Giga Group Pakistan, overseeing Goldcrest BaySands a landmark luxury residential tower at HMR Waterfront, Karachi, since March 2022. He leads the complete project lifecycle, including concept development, architectural design, pricing strategy, marketing, sales, payment structuring, and branding. Under his leadership, Goldcrest BaySands introduced Pakistan’s first resort-style apartments with private plunge pools, setting a new benchmark for luxury waterfront living in Karachi

Adnan returned to Pakistan after a distinguished 25-year career in the UAE, spanning Finance and Business Development across government and private sectors. He has a proven track record in complex financial transactions, fundraising, debt structuring, and investment advisory for large-scale infrastructure projects. His last assignment was with Dubai Aviation City Corporation (DACC), where he led fundraising of USD 32 billion and debt management for the development of Al Maktoum International Airport, Dubai South, and other aviation projects for the Government of Dubai.

He has successfully secured commitments from leading global financial institutions including Rothschild, JP Morgan, UniCredit Bank AG, CIMB Group, Santander, KfW, Standard Chartered, and Citi Bank, along with major Export Credit Agencies/EXIM Banks for the AMIA development. Adnan holds a Master’s degree in Finance and brings deep expertise in financial analytics, fund management, and cross-border transactions.

Mr. Adnan Saeed is a highly prominent figure in the business community and social networking circles. He possesses exceptional public relations and networking skills and is known as a highly wellconnected personality. He is currently serving as the President of the Rotary Club of Karachi Quaid.
